Who we are

A Word-centred assembly

We are a family of believers in Warri who hold the Bible to be the complete, infallible Word of God, and who believe that God has sent a prophetic Message in this last age to restore His people to that Word and make ready a Bride for the coming of Jesus Christ.

Whether you have walked with the Lord for decades, are searching honestly for truth, or are simply curious — you are welcome here.
