Who we are

A Word-centred assembly in Effurun

We are a family of believers in Effurun who hold the Bible to be the complete, infallible Word of God, and who believe that God has sent a prophetic Message in this last age to restore His people to that Word and make ready a Bride for the coming of Jesus Christ.

Whether you have walked with the Lord for decades, are searching honestly for truth, or are simply curious, you are welcome here.

From the pastor's desk

“The Word of God is not a tradition we inherited and keep out of habit. It is living, and it is able to find a man where he is and change him. That is why we open the Bible here without hurry.
