What happens in a service

We begin with singing and prayer. There is a time of worship, then the Word is preached — usually for an hour or more, because the teaching is the heart of what we do. We close in prayer. A Sunday service normally runs about three hours.

  • What should I wear?

    Modest, respectful dress. Most of the brothers wear shirts and trousers; the sisters wear dresses or skirts with their heads covered during service. You will not be turned away or singled out over clothing on your first visit.

  • Will I be put on the spot?

    No. Visitors are welcomed warmly, but nobody will be asked to stand, speak, or give. You are free to sit, listen, and leave when you wish.

  • What about my children?

    Children are welcome in the service with their parents. [Confirm whether there is a separate Sunday school and its times.]

  • Is there parking?

    [Confirm parking arrangements and nearest landmark for those arriving by keke or taxi.]

  • Do I need to bring anything?

    Bring your Bible if you have one. If you don't, that is no obstacle — come as you are.

  • What if I am not a believer?

    You are welcome. Many people first came in simply to listen. There is no pressure, and no requirement to believe anything before you arrive.
