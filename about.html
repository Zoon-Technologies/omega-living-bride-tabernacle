About us

Who we are

An end-time Message assembly in Effurun, Delta State, gathered around the Word of God.

In plain words

Omega Living Bride Tabernacle is a church in Effurun. We meet on Sundays and midweek to pray, to sing, and above all to hear the Bible taught.

We believe the Bible is the complete and infallible Word of God. We believe that God has always sent prophets to call His people back to that Word, and that in this last age He sent such a ministry through William Marrion Branham. He came not to add to Scripture, but to open it, and to make ready a people prepared for the return of Jesus Christ.

If that is unfamiliar to you, you are still welcome. You do not have to agree with us to sit with us. Come, listen, and search the Scriptures for yourself.

Prove all things; hold fast that which is good.1 Thessalonians 5:21

What we believe

  • The Word of GodThe Bible is the inspired, complete and infallible Word of God, and the final authority for all faith and conduct.
  • One GodThere is one God, who revealed Himself fully in Jesus Christ: God manifested in flesh.
  • Salvation in ChristSalvation is by grace through faith in the finished work of Jesus Christ at Calvary, and by no other means.
  • Water baptismBelievers are baptised by immersion in the Name of the Lord Jesus Christ.
  • The Holy SpiritThe baptism of the Holy Spirit is the promise of the Father to every believer.
  • The end-time MessageGod sent the prophetic ministry of Malachi 4:5–6 and Revelation 10:7 in this last age to restore the hearts of the children to the faith of the apostolic fathers.
  • The return of ChristJesus Christ is coming again, bodily and visibly, for a Bride made ready by His Word.

Our name

“Omega” speaks of the last: the final age of the church before the coming of the Lord. “Living Bride” speaks of what the Scripture calls the true church: not a building or an organisation, but a people joined to Christ as a wife to a husband. “Tabernacle” is the place of meeting, where God comes down to dwell among His people.
